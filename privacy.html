Privacy policy

Hermes Assistant is a personal, self-hosted tool used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit Google sign-in, the app reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar and can create Gmail drafts and calendar events. Every draft or event is created only after the owner approves it.

Where data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
